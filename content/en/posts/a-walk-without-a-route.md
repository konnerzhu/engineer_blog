---
title: A Walk Without a Route
date: 2026-09-04
description: On noticing more by deciding less before you leave the house.
categories: [Living]
tags: [attention, notes]
---

Some afternoons are better when the destination is left open. I take the first street that looks interesting, then the next one, and let the neighborhood make the itinerary.

Without a route to follow, small details start to stand out: a plant finding its way through a fence, a hand-painted sign, the warm light in a window just before evening.

I come home with no achievement to report, which is sometimes exactly what a day needs. Attention is its own kind of souvenir.
