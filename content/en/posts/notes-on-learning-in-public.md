title: Notes on Learning in Public
date: 2026-08-22
description: Sharing the rough edges of a learning process can make the work more useful to everyone.
categories: [Learning]
tags: [writing, learning]
---

When I write about something I have just learned, the explanation is usually more honest. I still remember which words were confusing and which examples made the idea click.

That is useful for a future version of me, but it can also help someone who is a few steps behind. A polished guide has its place. So does a careful account of the path that led there.

The trick is to label uncertainty clearly, link to good sources, and return to update the note when experience changes the answer.
